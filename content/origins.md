Nobody set out to start a Nix user group. People found Nix separately, for
their own reasons, and some of those paths happened to cross. Life has a
curious way of weaving our stories together long before we realize they are
connected. We rarely know who we will meet, where a chance encounter might
lead, or what adventures await us on the other side of a seemingly ordinary
decision. Strangers become acquaintances, familiar faces become friends, and
friendships grow into something that endures. As new people enter our lives,
the threads loosen, unravel, and weave themselves anew. Each new thread changes
the pattern, bringing people together in ways that were not possible before.
The tapestry is never finished, nor is its pattern predetermined. It is only
when we look back that we begin to see how intertwined our threads have become,
how seemingly insignificant moments shaped the opportunities before us, and how
something none of us set out to create became something we could not have
created alone.
